# Voice Patterns — Things to Remember

## The Admiral's Portland

- 2001, not 1992. Same energy, nine years later.
- Blue Parrot is a DIVE. Cash only, no register, gate always open, people go behind the bar.
- Mock's Crest is PROFESSIONAL. Register system with nicknames. Good food. Heavy pours. Video poker.
- Mouse Trap is DEAD. The people who show up are not to be messed with. Table out front, not a porch.
- Columbia Park is HUGE during the day — kids, dogs, community. At night — you're not supposed to be there. Period.

## Character Origins (from Admiral's stories)

- Maurice: real person, older Black man, charismatic deadbeat, magic tricks, life advice, dishwashing hustle. Name unknown IRL — "Maurice" workshopped with Admiral.
- Dennis: the Kiss fight. Regular got beat up by a minor with a fake ID on the Blue Parrot smoke porch. Greg (bartender) carded the kid AFTER the fight. Dennis never went back. Pretends it didn't happen.
- Tina: based on a real bartender who let people behind the bar, into the basement. Sweet, attractive, not sharp. Got fired.
- Greg: the other bartender. Had cameras. Broke up fights. The responsible one.
